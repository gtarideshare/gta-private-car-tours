Popular Tours & Itineraries

Every trip is private and flexible — start when you like, stop where you like, and change the plan on the day. The itineraries below are starting points; tell us your date and stops and we will build the day around you.

Day Trips from the GTA

FULL DAY · 8–9 HRS

Niagara Falls, Niagara-on-the-Lake & Wineries

The classic: Horseshoe Falls and Table Rock, the scenic Niagara Parkway, Whirlpool Rapids and the Floral Clock, then the old town of Niagara-on-the-Lake and a winery stop on the way home. An evening version catches the illuminated Falls and seasonal fireworks.

HALF TO FULL DAY

Toronto City Tour

CN Tower and the waterfront, Casa Loma, the Distillery District, St. Lawrence Market, Kensington Market and Queen West — ideal for visiting family, a first day in Toronto, or a layover with luggage safely in the car.

FULL DAY · 8 HRS

Blue Mountain & Wasaga Beach

Blue Mountain Village and the gondola in summer, skiing in winter, plus the world's longest freshwater beach at Wasaga — a four-season escape about two hours from the GTA.

FULL DAY · 9–10 HRS

Muskoka Cottage Country

Lake Muskoka towns — Gravenhurst, Bracebridge and Port Carling — lake lookouts, waterfalls and, in season, a lake cruise. Ontario's cottage country at its prettiest in summer and fall.

LONG DAY · 11–12 HRS

Algonquin Provincial Park

Highway 60 corridor trails, visitor centre and lake viewpoints. The fall-colours trip everyone asks about — go on a weekday if you can, and start early.

LONG DAY

Bruce Peninsula & Tobermory

The Grotto and crystal-clear Georgian Bay shoreline at the tip of the Bruce Peninsula. A long day, best May to October, with parking timing planned around your visit.

FULL DAY · 10 HRS

Kingston & Thousand Islands

Historic Kingston, then the Thousand Islands — island-studded St. Lawrence scenery and seasonal cruises from Gananoque or Rockport.

DAY TRIP OR ONE-WAY

Ottawa — Canada's Capital

Parliament Hill, the Rideau Canal, ByWard Market and the national museums. Book it as a long day trip, a 2-day stay, or a one-way drop-off.

Multi-Day Tours

2 DAYS

Montreal

Old Montreal and Notre-Dame Basilica, Mount Royal, St. Joseph's Oratory, the Old Port and real bagels and poutine — with an overnight stay so the city isn't rushed.

2 DAYS

Quebec City

North America's oldest walled city: the Old Town, Château Frontenac, Montmorency Falls and Île d'Orléans, at a relaxed two-day pace.

3 DAYS

Montreal + Quebec City

The most popular multi-day combination: two days of French Canada — Montreal first, then Quebec City — with scenic stops along the St. Lawrence.

4–6 DAYS

Eastern Canada Loop

Build a private loop: Toronto → Kingston & Thousand Islands → Ottawa → Montreal → Quebec City → Niagara Falls. Your car, your driver, your hotels — we plan the driving so you don't have to.

5+ DAYS

Atlantic Canada — the Maritimes

Halifax, Peggy's Cove, Lunenburg, Prince Edward Island and the Bay of Fundy. A true road trip, quoted individually — message us with your dates and wish list.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a tour cost?

There is no public price list: every trip is quoted by distance and time — how far you go and how long you need the car and driver. Message us your date, pickup point and destinations on WhatsApp (@unionville) or by DM, and we reply with one clear quote per car, not per person. Asking for a quote is free and carries no obligation.

What is included in the quote?

Your quote covers the private car and driver for your agreed itinerary. Unless your quote says otherwise, items such as highway tolls (including Highway 407), parking, attraction tickets, meals and — on multi-day trips — accommodation are extra. Everything is confirmed in writing before you book, so there are no surprises on the day.

How many passengers can ride?

The Camry carries up to 4 passengers. It is most comfortable with 1–3 passengers, especially for long-distance trips. Travelling with more people or lots of luggage? Message us and we will tell you honestly whether it fits.

Is this a shared tour or a guided tour?

It is a private car charter: the car and driver are yours for the trip — never shared with strangers. Your driver plans the route and stops with you and shares local knowledge along the way; entry tickets and any in-attraction guided tours are separate and arranged by you (we can point you to the official ticket channels).

Do I need to book far in advance?

For day trips, a few days ahead usually works, but weekends, holidays and fall-colours season fill up — earlier is better. Multi-day and long-distance trips (Montreal, Quebec City, Maritimes, New York, west coast) should be booked as early as you can so dates can be held.

What is the cancellation policy?

Life happens: tell us as early as possible. Your exact cancellation terms are confirmed in writing with your quote before you pay anything. For longer and multi-day trips, a deposit is normally required to secure your date — the deposit terms form part of that written confirmation.

Can you take us to the United States?

Yes — Buffalo and New York City trips are available. Crossing the border is every passenger's own responsibility: each passenger must carry valid documents for entering the US and returning to Canada. Please check your documents before booking.

Can you pick us up at Toronto Pearson Airport?

Yes. YYZ pickup and drop-off is available across the GTA, including rides straight between the airport and Niagara Falls or other destinations. Share your flight details when you message so pickup timing can be planned around your arrival.
